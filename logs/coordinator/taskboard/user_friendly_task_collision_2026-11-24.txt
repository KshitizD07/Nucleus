1. OBJECTIVE
   - Provide clear, actionable feedback when a task claim collision occurs or when an agent/contributor attempts to claim a task outside their active lane.
   - Return descriptive error messages showing who currently owns the claimed task and listing remaining available tasks in the contributor's lane.

2. WORK PERFORMED & ARCHITECTURE DETAILS
   - Updated `packages/cli/src/commands/tasks.ts` (`claimTaskLocally`):
     - On collision (task already claimed by another contributor):
       Outputs:
       "⚠️ Task [<task_id>] was already claimed by <assignee>."
       "Available tasks in '<lane>' lane: [<task_id_1>, <task_id_2>]"
     - On lane mismatch (contributor in lane A claiming task for lane B):
       Outputs:
       "⚠️ Lane mismatch: Task [<task_id>] belongs to '<task.lane>' lane, but your active lane is '<lane>'."
       "Available tasks in '<lane>' lane: [...]"
   - Updated `packages/coordinator/src/server/coordinator-server.ts` (`handleTaskClaim`):
     - When `this.taskBoard.claimTask()` returns `success: false`, the coordinator queries `this.taskBoard.getByLane(targetLane)` for unassigned tasks in the node's lane and includes `available_tasks` array in the `task:claim_failed` payload.
   - Updated `packages/cli/src/commands/join.ts`:
     - Handles incoming `task:claim_failed` events and prints available tasks directly to the contributor terminal.
   - Added unit test in `packages/cli/src/cli.test.ts` and integration verification in `packages/coordinator/src/server/critique.integration.test.ts`.

3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
   - When multiple autonomous agents or human contributors run simultaneously in a room, race conditions on task claims are natural. Raw boolean failures or generic "claim rejected" errors leave agents stuck or guessing which tasks they should work on next.
   - Returning the explicit available task list in the rejection response immediately unblocks the agent's next action.

4. WHY NOT THE ALTERNATIVE(S)
   - Silent auto-assignment to next task: Violates explicit intent and could assign a task the developer or LLM context was not prepared to take on.
   - Forcing another full board fetch over network: Adds an extra round-trip and race window. Including available tasks in the rejection envelope is atomic and zero-latency.

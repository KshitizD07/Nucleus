--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TASK OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Expanded Nucleus beyond 3-tier software engineering into complex research,
philosophical debate, and product strategy without disrupting existing
monorepo engineering workflows. Users can now run:
- nucleus init (default: engineering monorepo)
- nucleus init --preset dialectic --topic "Is consciousness algorithmic?"
- nucleus init --preset brainstorm --topic "Autonomous Dev Environments"

Enforces three strict dialectic rules:
1. Strict turn-taking: Neither side (Pro / Contra) may claim or reply until
   the opponent's preceding round is approved and completed.
2. Atomic conciseness: Hard 200-word limit per turn enforced by Critique Gate,
   preventing rambling essays and forcing step-by-step deconstruction.
3. Live visibility: Center / user observes both sides composing arguments in
   real-time via .nucleus/shadows/<lane>/ before rounds are even completed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ARCHITECTURAL & PROTOCOL DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Added "blocked" status to TaskStatusSchema in @nucleus/protocol.
- Automated dependency unblocking in TaskBoardManager.updateStatus():
  When round-01-pro transitions to "approved", round-01-contra automatically
  unblocks from "blocked" to "unassigned".
- Preset configuration architecture under packages/cli/src/presets/:
  - engineering: default, 100% backward compatible (frontend, backend, CONTRACT/api.md)
  - dialectic: pro, contra, judge; CONTRACT/topic.md, axioms.md, speaking_floor.md;
    5 atomic rounds with strict turn-taking dependencies
  - brainstorm: vision, skeptic, market, systems; CONTRACT/PRD.md, decisions.md
- Critique Gate Brevity Guard in StaticRuleCriticProvider:
  Counts words on added lines for pro/contra lanes. Flags critical finding
  if argument exceeds 200 words, returning action guidance to deconstruct.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Single-LLM prompts for debate or strategy suffer from sycophancy, context
smear, and balance bias. True dialectic progress requires multi-model adversarial
tension (e.g. Gemini vs. Claude vs. DeepSeek) with strict floor turns and
concise premise-by-premise deconstruction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHY NOT THE ALTERNATIVE(S)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Alternative: Hardcode pro/contra into coordinator server.
  Rejected: Would contaminate the core software engineering monorepo mission.
  Presets keep domain logic decoupled as modular profile templates.
- Alternative: Enforce turns via interactive terminal prompts.
  Rejected: Violates the Pure Pull Model and breaks autonomous AI agents.

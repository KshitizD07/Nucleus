--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. OBJECTIVE
   - Eliminate robotic AI headings (e.g., "**Premise 1:**", "**Rebuttal:**", "**Conclusion:**")
     in dialectic debate rooms, enforcing natural, persuasive human-like prose.
   - Implement dynamic, open-ended round spawning: instead of terminating at a fixed
     round count (like 2 or 5), the room dynamically spawns Round N+1 whenever Round N
     (Contra) is approved, continuing indefinitely until synthesis is achieved or 50 rounds.

2. WORK PERFORMED & ARCHITECTURE DETAILS
   - Dynamic Dialectic Spawner in `TaskBoardManager.updateStatus`:
     - Keys on regex `/^round-(\d+)-contra$/i`.
     - When `round-N-contra` achieves `approved` status, checks whether synthesis has been
       approved (`hasSynthesis`).
     - If not approved and `nextRoundNum <= 50`, dynamically instantiates:
       * `round-(N+1)-pro` (status: `unassigned`, depends on `round-N-contra`)
       * `round-(N+1)-contra` (status: `blocked`, depends on `round-(N+1)-pro`)
     - Extended `updateStatus` return signature to include `spawnedTasks?: TaskItem[]` and
       `unblockedTasks?: TaskItem[]`.
   - Real-Time Broadcast Integration:
     - Updated `CoordinatorServer.handleTaskStatusUpdate` and `runAutomatedCritique` to
       broadcast WebSocket `task:status` envelopes for all spawned and unblocked tasks.
   - Natural Dialogue Guard in Critic (`packages/critic/src/reviewer/ai-critic.ts`):
     - Added regex check `/\*\*(?:Premise|Counter-Premise|Rebuttal|Conclusion)(?:\s*\d*)?:\*\*/i`.
     - Flags artificial template headings and instructs agents to write in fluent,
       conversational prose speaking directly to their opponent.
   - Preset Guidance Overhaul (`packages/cli/src/presets/dialectic.ts`):
     - Replaced analytical template instructions with Oxford Union style conversational
       guidance: 100-180 words, vivid real-world analogies, direct counter-arguments.
   - Integration Test Suite:
     - Updated `packages/cli/src/cli.test.ts` to assert dynamic spawning of Round 2 tasks
       upon Round 1 Contra approval.

3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
   - User Feedback: Prior debates produced sterile, bullet-pointed academic definitions
     rather than engaging, authentic arguments.
   - Dynamic Continuation: Deep intellectual discoveries and resolutions cannot be
     artificially bounded by static 5-round limits. Continuous dynamic progression allows
     agents to drill deeper into core assumptions without manual user intervention.

4. WHY NOT THE ALTERNATIVE(S)
   - Alternative 1 (Pre-seeding 50 rounds statically in `tasks.json`): Rejected because it
     bloats the task board with hundreds of blocked tasks, wasting memory and confusing
     agents parsing `.nucleus/tasks.json`.
   - Alternative 2 (Manual CLI round creation by user): Rejected because the debate should
     flow autonomously as agents critique, refine, and advance arguments.

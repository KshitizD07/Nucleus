   - Implement Milestone 5 of the Nucleus architecture:

     * `nucleus init`: Automatically scaffolds `CONTRACT/` and `.nucleus/`, spins up the

   - Created `@nucleus/cli`:

     * `src/commands/init.ts` (`InitRunner`): Hosts room, scaffolds contracts and directories, issues invite tokens.
     * `src/commands/join.ts` (`JoinRunner`): Client runner orchestrating `DaemonClient`, `PeersIndexManager`,
       `ShadowMirror`, `ContractReplica`, `TaskBoardReplica`, and `WorkspaceDiffWatcher`.
   - Integration tests in `packages/cli/src/cli.test.ts`:
     * Verified CLI argument parser against valid, invalid, and help options.
     * Verified full programmatic E2E run: `InitRunner` hosting room on port 9015, issuing an invite,
       `JoinRunner` joining the room, and `.nucleus/PEERS.md` being rendered in the joiner workspace.

3. WHY BEHIND THE CHANGE
   - Zero-Friction Developer UX: Engineers and hackathon teams can now collaborate with two simple commands:
     `nucleus init` on the lead machine and `nucleus join ...` on teammate laptops.
   - Operating Under Pure Pull Model: The CLI runs as a background synchronization companion without ever
     spawning or wrapping the user's AI coding tool.
